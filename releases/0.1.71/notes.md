Your projects and folders from Claude, Grok, Perplexity, and Copilot now sync into your library.

## Added

- Projects and folders now sync from Claude, Grok (Projects), Perplexity (Spaces), and Copilot, joining ChatGPT. Conversations appear under their folder, and a folder deleted on the source never deletes your saved conversations.
- Copilot conversations inside a project are now captured at all: Copilot only lists them inside the project, so earlier versions never saw them.
- The sync view shows a dedicated projects step with live counts, and Sync History gains a Projects column.

## Improved

- Copilot now syncs only in browsers that can actually read it. Microsoft Edge blocks extensions on copilot.microsoft.com, so those doomed attempts are skipped instead of failing every run.
- The New Chat button moved from the sidebar into the top right of the chats page.

## Fixed

- Ask your archive no longer fails with "reached its round limit": the search now wraps up with its best answer from the evidence it gathered.
