Syncing keeps your place in the list, and a few rough edges in the sidebar are gone.

## Improved

- Headings across the app use one consistent weight and letter spacing.

## Fixed

- Syncing no longer interrupts the conversation list. Conversations appear as
  they are saved, and the list keeps your scroll position instead of jumping
  back to the top.
- Changing a source's visibility quickly no longer leaves an empty row behind
  in the sidebar.
- "All Sources" no longer appears selected when no source filter is set.
