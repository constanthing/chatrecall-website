Pages open instantly while a sync runs, sync activity lives on the Sync
row, and the new website Help page is one click away.

## Added

- A Help page on the ChatsRecall website with answers to common questions
  about setup, syncing, and your data, plus an Open Help page button in the
  app's Help & Support dialog that takes you straight there.
- A sync activity indicator on the Sync row of the sidebar: while a sync
  runs, a small orange ring appears at the right edge of the row, or on the
  corner of the sync button when the sidebar is collapsed. It is visible
  from every page of the app.

## Improved

- The status line in the sidebar's bottom corner now shows when your
  library last synced on every page. The conversation Reader, Extensions,
  and New Chat pages used to always say "Local".
- The search field shows a quiet accent border when focused instead of a
  thick outline ring.
- The Help & Support dialog closes with an X in its corner and fits its
  actions on one line.

## Fixed

- Opening a page while a sync was running could show a blank window for a
  second or two before anything appeared. Pages now show immediately and
  fill in as their content loads.

## Removed

- The orange "Syncing" pill in the sidebar's bottom corner. The Sync row's
  activity ring replaces it.
