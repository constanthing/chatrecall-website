Capture keeps up with the site changes that landed on Gemini, Perplexity,
ChatGPT and Wave.

## Fixed

- Gemini prompts are saved the way you typed them. Prompts captured since late
  August were stored twice over, with a cut-off copy stuck to the front.
- Gemini conversation titles no longer contain stray "&nbsp;" text. Titles
  already saved that way are repaired on first launch.
- Perplexity prompts are captured again after Perplexity redesigned its
  conversation page.
- Deleting a Perplexity thread from ChatsRecall removes it on Perplexity again.
- Conversations you delete on ChatGPT are recognized again, so they no longer
  linger in your library.
- Wave conversations load their titles again instead of stalling on the way in.
