Ask your archive handles long questions without stopping, and every answer
shows what it cost.

## Added

- Every answer from Ask your archive shows the tokens the run used, what it
  cost, and how long it took.
- A thumbs up or thumbs down under each answer sends feedback on whether it
  was useful.

## Fixed

- Ask your archive no longer stops partway through a long question with a
  token budget error. When a run does reach its ceiling, it answers from the
  evidence it already gathered and tells you it finished early.
