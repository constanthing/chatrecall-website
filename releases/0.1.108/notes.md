A feature release that reshapes two pages you use often: Settings and Moments.

## Highlights

- :sparkles: **A simpler Settings page** Settings opens on Start here: a box
  that says whether setup is done, or which things need your attention with a
  link to each, then eight short rows in the order you'll want them in your
  first hour, from the browser extension to getting help. Everything else is
  under Advanced.
- :list: **A short list of the agreements that still matter** Moments opens on
  a short list of what people agreed and still holds: promises, decisions made
  together, permissions, refusals, changes and advice. Everything else goes to
  a Diary tab, so nothing is thrown away, and each row lets you keep it on the
  short list, send it to the diary or hide it.

> Settings, Moments, Needs attention, Mac wording

## Added

- Appearance in Settings can be System, Light or Dark, and starts on System.
- The Moments short list shows a monthly budget line, a Small, still open
  group, and Life events, where you can add your own.

## Improved

- Background sync and the list of AI accounts are in Settings now, not on
  the Sync page.
- Find moments in my last 30 days reads meetings first, then imported
  conversations, then AI chats, says what it didn't read, and reads recent
  conversations again the next time you press it.
- Needs attention takes agreements only from the Moments short list, so one
  you send to the diary or hide stops counting there. Promises found before
  this update still count.
- On a Mac, Settings and the support report say this Mac, Finder, Keychain
  and Mac folder paths instead of Windows words.
