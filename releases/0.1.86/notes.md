Browse your chats by day, as cards or as a list, and hand a conversation to another app for a second opinion.

## Added

- List view is back. Switch between cards and a list with the buttons next to
  the page title. Each row shows the title and opening prompt on one line,
  with the project, prompt count and time beside them. Your choice is
  remembered.
- Chats are grouped by date: Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days, Previous 30
  days, then by month. New Chat is always the first item in Today.
- Continue in another app has two new modes. Give me a second opinion sends
  only your prompts, with the replies left out. Review this critically sends
  the whole conversation and asks what is wrong, weak, missing or risky in it.
- A short guided tour shows what changed the first time you open this version.

## Improved

- Pinned is now a filter. Turn on Pinned only in Filters, next to the search
  box, and combine it with a source, account, project or search to see, for
  example, only your pinned Gemini chats. It no longer takes a row in the
  sidebar.
- The search box buttons are labelled Ask and Filters.
- Cards are easier to scan. The date sits above the title where the source
  name used to be (the source icon still tells you where a chat is from), and
  inside Today and Yesterday it shows just the time. Previews that only
  repeat the title are hidden, and everything under the title lines up with
  it.
- The button next to Sources shows how many sources are hidden instead of a
  dot, and the sources window explains that hidden sources don't appear in
  Chats or search but keep syncing.
- Hover hints on icon buttons and cut-off names appear almost immediately and
  match the app's look.
- Continue in another app opens directly when only one browser extension can
  do it, and asks which browser to use otherwise. If the browser can't open
  it, the choice comes back with the reason.
- Handing off a conversation that was itself handed off no longer stacks
  instructions inside the title and prompts.

## Fixed

- Gemini conversations no longer miss prompts that were scrolled out of view,
  including turns that only contain an attachment.
- Deleting a conversation always removes it from ChatsRecall, even when a
  sync runs at the same moment.
- Continue in another app no longer mixes your text with a draft the other
  app restores in its message box. It waits for the box to settle and tells
  you if an earlier draft is still there.
