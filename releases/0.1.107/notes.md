A feature release: ChatsRecall now gathers your ideas and lists, and shows what needs your attention.

## Highlights

- :book: **Your ideas and lists, gathered for you** The Concepts tab now shows
  your own ideas, frameworks and terms, each with its meaning and every
  conversation it came up in, plus kept lists such as My friends or Places I
  want to travel to. Find concepts and lists in my last 30 days fills them
  from your recent conversations; list entries arrive as suggestions you keep
  or dismiss, and you can add your own.
- :alertCircle: **Needs attention** A new page under Insights shows what needs
  your notice now and why: promises that are overdue or nearly due, things
  you promised, things you're waiting on, and open or reversed decisions.
  Each one links to where it was said and has Done, Not mine and Snooze
  buttons. It makes no AI call, so it's free to open.

> concepts, assistants, meeting search

## Added

- Find concepts and lists works with your own OpenAI or Anthropic key or on
  the included AI, which gives it its own small share of the day so Ask
  archive keeps the rest.
- Connected assistants can list your concepts and lists, read a concept, add
  an entry to a list and ask what needs your attention.

## Improved

- Concepts found on the included AI are better, because they now come from
  Gemini 3.6 Flash.
- The old list of short phrases is still on the Concepts tab, collapsed
  under Search vocabulary.

## Fixed

- After an extra title was added, edited or removed on a meeting, searching
  for someone who was in it no longer found the meeting.
