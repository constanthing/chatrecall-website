A small release: fixes only, nothing new to learn.

## Fixed

- New Gemini prompts reach your library again as you send them. A change
  on Gemini's side in late October stopped the app from noticing a sent
  prompt, so new chats and follow-ups waited for the next sync.
- A follow-up in an existing Gemini chat is saved together with its reply,
  not before the reply has appeared.
