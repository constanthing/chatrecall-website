A big update: search, Ask and the Mac app all change in ways you will notice.

## Highlights

- :search: **Search by meaning** Search now also finds conversations that say
  the same thing in other words, so "car insurance renewal" can turn up a
  chat that only ever said "auto policy". It runs on this computer after a
  one-time 30 MB download, and Match by meaning in Filters turns it off.
- :sparkles: **Ask your archive with no setup** Every install gets a free
  courtesy AI allowance for 90 days, with nothing to sign up for or copy in.
  Settings shows when it ends and warns you three weeks before, and can get
  you a free Google Gemini key of your own.
- **A signed Mac app** The Mac version is now signed and checked by Apple,
  so it opens like any other app, without the Privacy & Security steps.

> search, meetings, sync, watched folders, agents

## Added

- Very common words get a "Which did you mean?" row above the results: one
  button per meaning, each with a count, so a search for "session" can be
  narrowed to the kind you meant.
- Search finds a meeting by who was in it, from the calendar invite, the
  notetaker's list or the speaker labels, even when the name is never said.
- A Commitments page finds the commitments, action items and goals in your
  conversations and shows whose move each one is. It needs an OpenAI key.
- Settings > Updates has an Email report button: it saves the support
  report, shows the file and opens an email in your own mail program, ready
  for you to attach it and send.
- Settings has a Local glossary section for the glossary search.
- A watched folder can download OneDrive files that are kept online only, so
  they can be imported. It is a per-folder option and off by default.
- The Help row in the sidebar shows the app version.
- TabSidecar can show related chats from ChatsRecall. It only searches, and
  it is off until you switch it on.

## Improved

- When a search finds few exact matches, it adds results for related words
  underneath them. Exact matches always stay on top.
- The Sync page names what happened for each source, and a sync that only
  got through part of the way says Partial.
- Claude Code and Cowork sessions on this computer are read every 5 minutes,
  and a busy database no longer stops a sync.
- A Claude Code session stored both on this computer and on claude.ai shows
  as one row.
- Due dates in Moments count from the conversation's own date, not from the
  day it was read.
- AI agents connected to ChatsRecall can ask your archive questions with the
  courtesy AI or your own Gemini key, choose how long excerpts are, and find
  people whose names are misspelled.

## Fixed

- A watched OneDrive folder no longer reports files that were already
  imported and later freed up to save space.
- A higher daily limit or a later end date for the courtesy AI now reaches
  the app, and a courtesy key that stops working is replaced at the next
  start even if ChatsRecall was closed in between.
- The courtesy AI key is saved on a Mac too.
