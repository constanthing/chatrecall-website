A small release: one date fix, and ChatsRecall is back on the Mac.

## Highlights

- :sparkles: **ChatsRecall for Mac** The Mac version is available again from
  the website, for Apple-chip and Intel Macs, and it updates itself from now
  on like the Windows version.

> imported dates, Mac setup and updates

## Improved

- On a Mac, first-run setup finds the AI apps you have installed, and
  Connect an assistant offers Claude Desktop's own settings file.
- A Mac that has no update waiting now says so, instead of showing an
  update error.

## Fixed

- Conversations added with Import or from a watched folder show their real
  date again. Builds up to 0.1.102 saved some of them with a date far in the
  future; they are corrected the next time ChatsRecall opens.
