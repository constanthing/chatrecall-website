A small repair release, nothing new to learn.

## Highlights

- :srcChatgpt: **Caught up with ChatGPT's redesigned page** ChatGPT has
  started giving some accounts a new page. New chats started on it are saved
  the moment you send them, and Continue in another app fills its new
  message box.

> new ChatGPT chats, Continue in another app

## Fixed

- A chat you start on ChatGPT's redesigned page is saved as soon as you send
  its first prompt, as on the old page. Since the redesign it was picked up
  only at its second prompt or at the next sync, so a chat started from a
  ChatsRecall project could miss its project and not open in the Reader.
- Continue in another app fills ChatGPT's new message box. On the redesigned
  page it had stopped with "the ChatGPT message box did not appear".
