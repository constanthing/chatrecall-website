Find any word inside a long conversation, and judge search results at a glance.

## Added

- Find in conversation. Press Ctrl+F in the Reader, or click the magnifier in
  its header, to search the conversation you are reading. Enter and
  Shift+Enter step through the matches with a count of where you are, and Esc
  closes the bar. Opened from a search result, it starts filled with your
  search.
- Sort Classic search by relevance. The Newest first / Relevance choice in the
  search options now works for Classic as well as Ladder.
- AI assistants connected through the local API or MCP can ask your archive a
  question and get a cited answer, the same one the Ask archive button gives.
  This needs a token with the new "ask" permission.

## Improved

- Search excerpts show more of the text around the match.
- Cards from today and yesterday show the time, such as "Today, 2:14 PM", so
  same-day conversations are easy to tell apart.
- New installs open in a larger window, and Settings > Reset UI > Minimize
  View returns to that size.

## Fixed

- Imported transcripts and Zoom meetings now show up in the library and in
  search. Before, they were saved but never shown.
- Opening a search result now highlights every match in the conversation and
  scrolls to the right one, including when you click anywhere on the card
  rather than on an excerpt.
- A result card with no matching excerpt no longer bolds unrelated words in
  its preview.
