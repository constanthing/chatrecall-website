Take a conversation with you: open it again in another app, already written
into the message box.

## Added

- Continue in another app. The Reader has a new action next to Open Original.
  Pick where to continue and ChatsRecall opens a new chat there with the
  conversation already in the message box, as one draft. Nothing is sent: you
  read it over and press send yourself. Destinations are ChatGPT, Claude,
  Claude Code, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, and Google AI Mode. Any
  conversation can be the starting point, including Cowork and Claude Code
  sessions that only exist on this computer.
- Hand over the whole conversation or a summary of it. The full transcript
  goes across as prompts and replies, capped at 50,000 characters: a longer
  conversation keeps its most recent messages and tells you how many earlier
  ones were left out. The summary is written by a model (the goal, what was
  decided, what is still open, where to pick up) and needs an OpenAI or
  Anthropic key in Settings; without one that choice is disabled with the
  reason on it.
- A conversation that belongs to a ChatsRecall project carries the project
  with it: the chat you continue is filed there as soon as you send it.

## Improved

- The browser connector's popup is a flat panel in the app's own colors now,
  following your system's light or dark setting. Connection status is one row
  that says what to do if it is down, sources waiting for a permission are
  plain rows with an Allow button, and the command history moved to a Logs
  link in the footer.
- A pasted-in conversation keeps its line breaks everywhere. Perplexity's
  message box used to fold the whole text into a single line, so it was
  offered only as a disabled destination; with browser extension 0.1.9 it
  keeps every line and blank line, and joins the list.
