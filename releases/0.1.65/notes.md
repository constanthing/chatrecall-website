Decide which sources fill your sidebar, and a temporary change of source icons.

## Added

- Pick which sources appear in the sidebar. A new button next to the SOURCES
  label opens a picker where you turn sources on or off. Hidden sources keep
  syncing, their conversations stay under All Sources, and search still finds
  them; only the sidebar row is hidden.
- The onboarding tour has a new step that introduces the sources list and the
  visibility picker.

## Improved

- The sidebar starts with six everyday sources visible (ChatGPT, Claude,
  Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, and Copilot). The rest can be turned on from the
  picker at any time. Nothing about your saved conversations changes.
- The source icons across the app have been temporarily changed to a new set.
