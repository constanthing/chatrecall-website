A feature release: conversations get extra titles, and Moments can look back over your last month.

## Highlights

- :search: **Give a conversation several titles** Right-click a conversation
  and choose Add a title here to name the part you're reading. It can carry
  up to seven extra titles, and search finds it by any of them and opens it
  at that part.
- :sparkles: **Find moments in your last 30 days** A new button on the
  Moments & X-Ray page reads up to 60 recent conversations at a time and
  pulls out their moments, including a new kind: commitments, who promised
  what and by when. It works with your own OpenAI or Anthropic key or on the
  included AI, which keeps a separate share for it so Ask archive is never
  used up.
- :link: **Connect any MCP assistant, Hermes included** Connect an agent has
  an Other assistant choice with ready-to-copy connection details, an
  mcpServers entry and a Hermes entry.

> groups, moments, the included AI, Mac wording

## Added

- Right-click a conversation and choose Add to group to see a suggested
  group. The suggestion also shows on the conversation's card, with a tick to
  accept it and a cross to dismiss it.
- Moments has commitments and outcomes filter chips.
- A moment can be backed by a document, a record or your own word, not just
  a quote, and a decision can become a lasting conclusion with dates for when
  it was true.

## Improved

- Every choice under Connect an agent uses Mac wording on a Mac, naming
  Terminal and TextEdit.
- The included AI accepts a sign-up end date of up to two years.
