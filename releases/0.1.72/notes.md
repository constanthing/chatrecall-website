Your archive gains two indexes over what it already holds: the terms it talks about, and the things that actually happened in it.

## Added

- Concepts: a browsable index of the topics, people, companies and products your archive talks about. Pick a term to see every conversation that mentions it, and open one straight at the message the term was found in. It is built entirely on your machine, with no model and no network call.
- Moments: an index of what happened rather than what was discussed. Decisions made, advice given, requirements set, conclusions reached, each quoted from the message it happened in and openable at that message. An X-Ray view lays a conversation's moments out in order and shows what superseded, refined, resolved or contradicted what, across conversations. Indexing uses the OpenAI key you added for search, runs only when you ask for it, and tells you the cost first.
- Import a transcript by hand, from Settings. A VTT or SRT file, or pasted text, becomes an ordinary conversation you can read, search and index like any other. It closes the gap where a recording was deleted at the vendor before anything ever captured it.
- Pin or unpin a conversation at its source from the Reader, for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Copilot and Wave.
- Folders from Wave now sync into your library.
- Sources you never use can be hidden from the sidebar, without changing anything about what syncs.
- The Sync page gains a Select All button, and its status dots are half again as large.

## Improved

- Ladder search and Ask your archive are ordinary features now. There is nothing to switch on first.
- The sidebar reads as three groups and is the same list on every page, so a row is never missing just because of the page you are on. Extensions and Settings moved down to the footer, above Help.
- Gemini replies are stored as Markdown, so lists, bold, code and tables survive into the Reader.
- Pinning and deleting at the source report their progress and their outcome in one status card, instead of a message that arrives before the work has finished.
- Pinning and deleting keep working after a restart, instead of failing until the next sync has run.
- A Copilot conversation inside a project now explains why it cannot be pinned, rather than trying and failing. Copilot has no pin for those conversations at all.

## Fixed

- A browser tab that had quietly stopped responding no longer reports itself as a sign-in problem.
- Moments no longer stop partway through a long conversation.

## Removed

- The Experimental switch at the bottom of Settings is gone. Everything it used to gate now ships on.
